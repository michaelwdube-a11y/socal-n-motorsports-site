Program questions

Is insurance included?

Program insurance is included in the bundled offering. Coverage, exclusions, and driver responsibilities are defined in the final agreement.

Which events are available?

Programs target Zenith and eligible GRIDLIFE events, subject to scheduling, entries, event rules, and vehicle readiness.

Are practice days available?

Yes. Practice days and selected single-day events can be arranged as focused development opportunities.

Are hill climbs available?

Yes. Hill-climb participation can be discussed when the event, vehicle configuration, and driver preparation align.

Event and series names describe intended participation only. Availability is not guaranteed, and no affiliation or endorsement by Zenith, GRIDLIFE, or their organizers is implied.
