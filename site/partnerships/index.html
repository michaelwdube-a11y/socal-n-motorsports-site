Partnership questions

Are partnership packages fixed?

No. The scope is built around the partner's objective, available events, deliverables, and what can be credibly executed.

Can a partner support the racing platform?

Yes. Technology collaboration is relevant across data, video, connectivity, infrastructure, AI, and trackside operations.

Do you work with drivers and teams?

Yes. Beta collaboration can use real telemetry, video, and workflow feedback when data rights and expected outputs are clear.

Are event organizers potential partners?

Yes. Organizers can help validate workflows involving race data, broadcast media, driver information, event operations, and audience value.
