THE PROBLEM

Race information is valuable only when it stays connected.

Timing, telemetry, video, notes, setup information, driver feedback, and media commonly live in separate systems. That fragmentation makes analysis slower and allows hard-earned knowledge to disappear between events.

The SoCal N Motorsports platform is being developed to preserve the relationships between the driver, car, lap, corner, event, and evidence—then make the next decision easier to understand.

01

Capture

Ingest telemetry, timing, onboard and broadcast video, vehicle data, notes, and race media with source context intact.

02

Correlate

Connect each signal to the correct event, session, car, driver, lap, corner, and piece of supporting evidence.

03

Improve

Produce coaching, engineering, operational, and media outputs that point back to real evidence instead of generic conclusions.

Beta questions

What stage is the platform in?

It is a working engineering prototype progressing toward a demonstration-grade MVP using real race telemetry and video.

What inputs are useful?

AIM or other vehicle logs, in-car video aligned to the data, timing information, broadcast footage, and reliable driver, car, track, and session metadata.

Who should participate?

Drivers, teams, organizers, and technology partners willing to test real workflows and evaluate whether the output improves a real decision.

What does a beta participant receive?

The exact output depends on usable source material and the current development milestone. Scope and expected deliverables are agreed before data is provided.
