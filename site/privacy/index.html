Privacy notice

Effective August 25, 2026. This notice describes how SoCal N Motorsports handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

The inquiry form collects the name, email address, selected area of interest, and message that you choose to submit.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to respond to inquiries, understand program interest, and continue a requested business conversation. SoCal N Motorsports does not sell inquiry information.

Form processing

The website is statically hosted through GitHub Pages. Form submissions are transmitted through a third-party form-processing service so that they can be delivered to SoCal N Motorsports. Do not submit confidential, financial, medical, or other highly sensitive information through the website form.

Analytics

The site may use Google Analytics after measurement is activated. Analytics can collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, and interactions with site controls. This information is used to understand site performance and improve the visitor experience.

External services

Hosting, form processing, analytics, and linked websites operate under their own privacy practices. Links to external services do not imply control over those services.

Retention and requests

Inquiry information is retained only as reasonably needed for the conversation, business records, security, and legal requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion, use the website inquiry form and select “Something else.”

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and services evolve. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.
