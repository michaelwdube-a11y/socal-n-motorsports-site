Track Intelligence Pack

This mock pre-event brief illustrates the product structure. It does not contain authoritative track instructions and all corner labels/objectives are fictional examples.

Session 1 objective

Establish safe, repeatable references and capture a clean baseline. Do not optimize every corner at once. The fictional plan below uses three learning priorities.

Corner preparation

ZoneQuestion to answerData to capture
High-speed entryWhat reference makes the entry repeatable?Speed, brake, throttle, steering if available
Long exitWhere can throttle become continuous?Throttle trace + onboard
Heavy brakingIs time lost on entry or release?Brake pressure/decel + speed

Capture checklist

  • Verify logger time/date and session naming
  • Start onboard video before leaving pit lane
  • Record tire pressures and major setup changes
  • Write three driver notes immediately after session

Debrief prompts

Confidence

Which corner felt least repeatable and why?

Change

What single adjustment had the clearest effect?

Evidence

What do we need to compare before session two?

Safety note: Official track rules, flags, event briefings, instructor direction, and vehicle limits always override a preparation document.
